About ARCSET

The people who plan it sell it, and see it through to go-live.

Entering Japan, or starting something new inside it, rarely fails on the idea. It fails in the handoffs: between the strategy and the salesperson, between the deal and the delivery team, between headquarters and the local office.

ARCSET removes those handoffs. The same team that designs the route to market opens the customer conversations, localizes the offer, and stays in the room until the system is running and the numbers are reported. We work in Japanese, English and Chinese, and we are comfortable on both sides of the table.

  • 1

    Cross

    Borders between countries, industries, organizations and technologies. We understand the expectations on both sides and translate between them.

  • 2

    Structure

    Advice becomes a plan with owners, deliverables, milestones and a reporting rhythm. Nothing stays as a slide.

  • 3

    Finish

    We stay through customer development, implementation, reporting and iteration until the business is running on its own.

    Leadership

    Sean GaoFounder and Representative Director

    Twenty years on the seam between Japan and the rest of Asia. First advising, then dealing, then operating.

    Sean came to Japan straight after university, nearly twenty years ago, and has spent his whole career where Japanese companies meet the outside world. At ABeam Consulting he learned how Japanese enterprises choose, buy and run software. At Deloitte Tohmatsu, over nine years and rising to Vice President, he moved to the deal side of that seam: cross-border M&A, joint ventures and fundraising between Japanese and Chinese companies, where the distance between a signed term sheet and a working business is widest.

    In 2025 he crossed to the operating side, co-founding the Japanese subsidiary of a voice AI company as Country Manager and taking it from zero to its first enterprise customers and partner network. ARCSET, founded in 2026, is the conclusion he drew from both halves: the plan, the sale and the build belong in the same hands.
